# Lantern Deep FAQ

Your question is not here? [Ask it, or report a bug or idea](https://github.com/acetousk/lantern-deep/issues/new/choose).

## Questions

**How do I attack?**
Walk into a monster. Each bump is one attack.

**What does a potion do?**
The colors change every run. Drink one to learn its name. After that, its name shows under the slot.

**How do I get my health back?**
Level up (kill monsters for XP), or rest: press **Wait** (Space, A, or the Wait button) when no monster is near.

**How do I win?**
Find the stairs on floors 1 and 2. Grab the Ember Relic on floor 3.

**Can I play with friends?**
Yes, 2–4 players. Pick **Host a room** and send the 4-digit code. Friends pick **Join a room** and type it. The host presses Start. See "Known problems" first.

## Known problems

**Host and Join say "Online co-op isn't ready yet".**
The online keys are not set up yet. Play solo for now. Co-op works only from the Godot editor (2 windows).

**The web page shows an old version.**
Press **Ctrl+Shift+R** on the game page.
